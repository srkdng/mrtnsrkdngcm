Boutique research in media, finance and politics.
Answering questions and uncovering hidden risk across borders.

When lecturing at ELTE University in Budapest, I tell my students that the best investigators can answer almost anything with just a spreadsheet, a notebook, and a phone. I speak from 15 years on the frontlines of investigative journalism in Hungary, exposing cross-border financial crime, corruption, and the illicit financing of international far-right networks. Operating under intense authoritarian pressure, every detail I published had to withstand strict legal scrutiny, always. I later brought this expertise to London’s corporate risk intelligence sector and currently lead financial research into digital news media and the European fact-checking ecosystems for a Budapest-based consultancy.

Complex problems rarely fit into neat boxes, so I mix and match methods to find the most pertinent answer to any research question. I am well versed in the usual staple of investigators – navigating corporate registries, media archives, digital breadcrumbs, cultivating expert sources and conducting high-stakes investigative interviews. But I am also conversant in the language of corporate accounts on the back of a strong numerical background as well as an in-depth understanding of both licit and illicit finances. I leverage over a decade of cross-border experience and deep Central and Eastern European cultural context to solve tough problems.

Recent Work

Capital Stacks of Journalism: Research Lead

Led an 8-month research effort into ~30 news media organisations for a cross-European financial benchmark analysis by the Center for Sustainable Media, a Budapest-based think tank and consultancy in the field of media finance. Mixed OSINT methods with the financial analysis of corporate accounts to clean and compare financial data sourced from over 20 jurisdictions.

Uncovering Major Corruption in Hungary: Public-Interest Reporting

After breaking a major national story via FOIA-ing and analysing hundreds of documents pertaining to the spending of hundreds of millions of euros in government advertising contracts together with Atlatszo colleagues, I traced some of the profits to a company acquiring the very same building that was later rented by the government to house the state communications agency. Read the full story (Hungarian) on Atlatszo.

Corporate Risk Intelligence: Research and Reporting

Boutique research and reporting services for a European private client interested in the business and political outlooks of a major, but politically contentious, Hungarian company. The research yielded understanding that formed the primary basis of a significant investment decision.

Reputational Due Diligence and Strategic Advisory

Boutique reputational due diligence research into possible business partners of a Hungarian company expanding abroad. A task initially focusing on research expanded to entail strategic advisory in consulting on the legal structure of a joint venture.

Uncovering Fidesz-MAGA connections: Public-Interest Reporting

On the back of years of reporting on the Hungarian public funds diverted to international far right networks by way of the government-financed Batthyány Lajos Foundation, this investigation revealed the background of the negotiations between the leadership of Hungary's now defunded Danube Institute and their MAGA connections. Read the full story (English) on Atlatszo.

Another recent investigation revealed the financing flows behind a Brussels-based English-language news site leading back to Fidesz. Read the full story (English) on Atlatszo.

Polish-Hungarian Far-Right networks: Public-Interest Reporting

After helping the investigation of the Polish public broadcaster into the life in Budapest of former deputy justice minister and current fugitive-from-justice Marcin Romanowski with my own research, I've extended the investigation to further reveal the numerous politically linked actors surrounding Romanowski in Budapest. (The former politician reportedly resides in Transnistria as of September 2026).

Read the full story (Hungarian) on Atlatszo. I've also investigated in further stories the direct political ties behind one of Romanowski's close Budapest connections, and the Hungarian state-backed funding for Romanowski's purported "research institute".
